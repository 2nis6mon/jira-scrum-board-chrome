Version 0.41.1 — Multiple active sprints and card menu

• Multiple active sprints: their goals are shown together, prefixed with their sprint name, with a Sprint filter. A sprint from another board, brought in by a shared issue, is flagged.
• Atlassian Goals pinned for the sprint of your choice when the board has several active sprints.
• New "Carried over" quick filter: issues carried over from a previous sprint of the board.
• Right-click a card (or Shift+F10): "Send to a goal", whose list opens on hover, "Remove from goal", "Move to top priority" and "Move to low priority". Jira's Priority field is not changed.
• Safer writes: linking an issue no longer removes what a teammate added in Jira in the meantime, two actions on the same board run one after the other, and signing out clears all local data for the site.
• Issues carried over when a sprint starts are no longer wrongly marked as added. The change log follows the chosen language.

No new Chrome permission.
