Find it in the user guide ↗
How do I choose the extension language?+

In Auto mode, the interface is French when your Jira is in French, and English for all other languages. Choose Auto, Français or English in personal preferences, available from administration or the site menu in the extension. The change is instant and leaves your issues, goals and statuses in their original language.

Does it work with my Kanban board?+

Yes. The extension uses your Kanban board’s filter and columns, with goals from Atlassian Goals. In Scrum, you can also use individual lines from the sprint goal text.

Do I need to set up the whole board again?+

No. Columns, statuses and issue types are read from Jira. You can then customize swimlanes and cards. Settings already shared by your team are picked up automatically.

Does it change my Jira permissions?+

No. Actions still follow your Jira permissions. Shared settings require board editing access, and transitions follow your workflow.

How do updates work?+

Chrome updates the extension automatically from the Chrome Web Store. Restarting your browser can help apply a pending update. On a company-managed browser, your administrator’s policies apply.
